Why marketers use Gmail dot variations

Gmail ignores dots before the @, so johnsmith@gmail.com, john.smith@gmail.com and jo.hnsmith@gmail.com all deliver to the same inbox. That gives you many test identities without creating new accounts.

How it works

Paste your address

Any gmail.com or googlemail.com address works, and so does a Google Workspace address at your company domain. Existing plus tags are kept.

Get every variation

The Dots tab lists every dot placement, with the easiest-to-read one pinned at the top as the best pick. The +Tags tab adds +alias versions like name+signup@gmail.com, which also land in your inbox.

Copy what you need

Click a row to copy one address, use Copy all for the whole tab (one per line, ready for a sheet), or download everything as a CSV.

FAQ

Can I test my full email funnel with one inbox?

Yes. Use different Gmail dot variations to trigger each path, while all test emails arrive in a single Gmail inbox.

How does this help when my team is QA-ing automations?

Each teammate can use a unique Gmail alias variation to test separate journeys at the same time and compare outcomes.

Can I use this for form tracking, tags, and segmentation QA?

Yes. Submit multiple Gmail dot trick aliases through forms to verify tags, list assignments, and branching logic.

Does it work for Outlook, Yahoo or iCloud?

No. Only Gmail and Google Workspace ignore dots in an address. Some other providers support +tags, but this tool is built for Google inboxes.
